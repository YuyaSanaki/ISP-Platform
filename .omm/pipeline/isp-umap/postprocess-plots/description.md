### English

Optional downstream plots: cluster/cell-type annotation, joint overlays, cell-type trajectories, L2 by coarse cell type. Switched on by `postprocess.enabled` (`core/postprocess_switch.py`), the same switch that runs rank enrichment; cells left Unknown or Ambiguous are drawn in grey in joint overlays.

### 日本語

任意の後処理の図です。クラスタと細胞種の付与、重ね合わせ図、細胞種ごとの軌跡、粗い細胞種ごとの L2。`postprocess.enabled`（`core/postprocess_switch.py`）でオンになり、rank enrichment と同じスイッチです。重ね合わせ図では Unknown や Ambiguous のままの細胞を灰色で描きます。
